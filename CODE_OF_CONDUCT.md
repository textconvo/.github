# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TextConvo maintains these repositories as a professional workspace. Everyone who takes part &mdash; maintainers, contributors, customers, and casual passers-by &mdash; deserves a harassment-free experience regardless of experience level, background, identity, or the language they code in.

We follow the spirit and the standards of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is worth reading in full.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and generous with people. Critique the change, not the author.
- Assume competence and good intent, and ask questions before assigning blame.
- Accept that maintainers must sometimes decline a contribution, and explain why when we do.
- Respect the scope of a repository. Off-topic threads get closed, not argued.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any form.
- Publishing another person’s private information, including phone numbers, addresses, or message contents.
- Sustained disruption: brigading, spam, repeated off-topic posting, or deliberate derailment.
- Posting real customer data, API keys, or personal data in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Scope

This policy applies in every TextConvo repository &mdash; issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and release notes &mdash; and to anyone representing TextConvo in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately through [textconvo.ai/contact-us](https://textconvo.ai/contact-us) with the subject line **Code of Conduct**. Include links and, where possible, screenshots.

Reports are read only by the maintainers responsible for this account. We will acknowledge receipt, investigate proportionately, and keep the reporter’s identity confidential. Do not use a public issue to report a concern.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, maintainers may respond with:

1. **A private correction** &mdash; a note explaining what was inappropriate and what we expect instead.
2. **A warning** &mdash; with a defined period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **A temporary ban** &mdash; from all TextConvo repositories and discussions.
4. **A permanent ban** &mdash; for sustained, severe, or retaliatory behaviour.

Maintainers who do not uphold this policy in good faith face the same consequences as anyone else.
