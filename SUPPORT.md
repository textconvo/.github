## Before you open an issue

1. Check [textconvo.ai/docs](https://textconvo.ai/docs) &mdash; the website is the source of truth and is updated first.
2. Search existing issues on the repository, including closed ones.
3. Confirm the problem is with the public example or specification rather than your account configuration.

## Writing a useful issue

Include the repository and file, the language and runtime version, what you expected, what happened, and the request and response with secrets redacted. Never paste a real API key, source key, webhook secret, customer phone number, or message body.

## Response expectations

GitHub issues are handled by the engineering team on a best-effort basis during business hours (Pacific Time). Production incidents and anything account-specific are always faster through [textconvo.ai/contact-us](https://textconvo.ai/contact-us).

A public status page and changelog are on the roadmap. Until they ship, follow the [blog](https://textconvo.ai/blog) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/textconvo/) for release notes.
